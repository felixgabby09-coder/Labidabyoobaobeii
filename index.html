To My Dearest Baba 💌

“I hate you. I don’t love you anymore. Just leave. I never should’ve forced us. Goodbye, I hope to never see you again.” These are words you will never hear me say. To my dearest baba, I love you. The affection I carry with me was molded in murky waters and heavy rain. It grew and grew, without stopping. I am one for words, but the breath you take away whenever I see you takes away the right of speech from my body, only being able to express it through my love. I know I love to show you I care with my words, but I believe for me, this time, simplicity wins. It matters not how I try to map out my feelings, just a single kiss could blow it all away. I love you baba, I love you.
